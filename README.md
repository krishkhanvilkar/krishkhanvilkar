### Hi, I'm Krish.

I'm building **[Seedr](https://seedr.space)**: the people you should build with, found for you.

Drop in your GitHub handle, a CV or a LinkedIn PDF. Seedr reads what you've shipped and drafts your card, no forms. Every morning at 8, a **Drop** brings up to five founders, startups or ideas that fit you, each with the reason. **Spark** drafts the first message, and you press send. Rough ideas go on the **Ideas** board, where builders tap "I'd build this".

Live in closed beta. Next.js, Supabase with row-level security on every table, PostHog.

**How it got here.** Seedr v1 was equity crowdfunding. Before writing more code, I stress-tested it against SEBI regulations and the IT Act. As designed, it had no legal path to launch. So I rebuilt it as a non-transactional discovery layer: no money moves through Seedr, and the core idea survived.

**Before Seedr.** Technical PM and AI orchestrator at recruit.ai: n8n multi-agent screening flows, spec to production in 14 days.
